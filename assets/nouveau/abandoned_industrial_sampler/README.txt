Abandoned Industrial Interior Kit, free sampler of 20 models

A small spread of models from Abandoned Industrial Interior Kit to try in your own project before you buy the full pack.
FBX and GLB hold the same models. Textures sit in the folders next to them. Y up, 1 unit = 1 m.
Each material slot is named after its texture set, so slot X uses the maps whose name contains X.
The maps in this sampler are 1024 px; the full pack has 2048 px maps.
